# Environmental Studies v2.2.1 — English–Tamil Semantic Parity Register

## Scope
This register records substantive parity/editorial corrections against the exact audited v2.2.0 content. It does not imply sentence-by-sentence translation. Scientific meaning, definitions, mechanisms, examples, values, tables and figure meaning are the parity criteria.

### Global corrections applied to every lesson
- Removed the English and Tamil `learning_outcome` blocks from authoritative content data.
- Removed generic Foundation/Core/Higher and Tamil level scaffolds from the concept flow.
- Replaced generic `தமிழில் விளக்கம்` headings with lesson-specific Tamil headings.
- Removed repeated heading text from body openings.
- Applied the controlled Tamil terminology master contextually rather than blind replacement.
- Preserved validated numerical, legal and regulatory content unless a documented correction required a change.
- Retained separate English and Tamil learning sections.

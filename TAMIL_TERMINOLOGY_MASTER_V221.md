# Environmental Studies v2.2.1 — Tamil Terminology Master

## Governing rule
Use Tamil Nadu State Board / Samacheer science and Bio-Zoology terminology first where an established term exists. Where a school-textbook term needs greater undergraduate precision, retain the Tamil term and introduce the standard English technical term in parentheses on first occurrence. Tamil prose must be natural scientific Tamil, not word-for-word translation.

This master governs lesson titles, prose, summaries, tables, captions, SVG labels, review material, assessment text and glossary-style references.

## Style rules
1. Tamil term first; English technical term in parentheses at the first useful occurrence.
2. Do not repeatedly append English to every subsequent occurrence.
3. Prefer complete Tamil clauses over label fragments.
4. In action diagrams, use grammatical imperatives: “வரைபடமிடு”, “பகுப்பாய்வு செய்”, “கண்டறி”, “கண்காணி”.
5. Avoid mixed English/Tamil place names in running Tamil prose when a familiar Tamil name exists: **இமயமலை (Himalaya)** on first occurrence, then **இமயமலை**.
6. Use consistent punctuation and Tamil spacing; avoid English sentence structure transplanted into Tamil.
7. Legal proper names may retain their official English/Indian statutory title where translation could create legal ambiguity.

Privacy Policy

Environmental Studies is designed to work offline. The app does not require an account and does not request camera, microphone, location, contacts, phone, SMS, or storage permissions.

Language preference, reading position and similar learning preferences may be stored locally on the user's device. This information is used only to preserve the learning experience and is not transmitted by the app.

This release contains no analytics, advertising SDK, tracking SDK, remote content loader, or in-app network request. The bundled textbook and interactive tools run locally.

App-local data can be removed by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

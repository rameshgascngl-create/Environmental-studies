# Environmental Studies v2.2.2 — Tamil Terminology Master

## Governing editorial rule
Use established Tamil Nadu school-textbook scientific terminology where it is precise and current, but write the lesson prose at **undergraduate/college register** rather than school coaching register. Tamil must read as original scientific Tamil, not word-for-word translation from English. Standard English technical terms, internationally used abbreviations, treaty names and statutory names may be retained in parentheses or tables where they improve precision.

This master governs Tamil lesson titles, prose, summaries, tables, captions, SVG labels and field-learning instructions.

## College-register rules
1. Replace coaching-style prompts such as **“நினைவில் கொள்க”** with academically neutral structures such as **“முக்கியக் கருத்து”**.
2. Use **“முக்கியக் கலைச்சொற்கள்”**, **“சிந்தனை வினா”**, **“பாடச்சுருக்கம்”**, **“விரிவான விளக்கம்”**, **“படக்கருத்து”** where pedagogically appropriate.
3. Avoid machine-concatenated fragments and colon-heavy telegraphic prose. Diagram explanations must be complete scientific sentences.
4. Explain causal chains explicitly: source → pathway → exposure → effect; nutrient enrichment → algal growth → decomposition → oxygen depletion; hazard + exposure + vulnerability → risk.
5. Do not translate internationally fixed abbreviations or legal/treaty proper names merely to make the page look more Tamil. Translate the concept; preserve the identifier where precision requires it.
6. Field-learning procedures may use nominal academic labels in diagrams and complete procedural prose in the lesson body.
7. English is permitted only when it serves a technical, statutory, measurement, proper-name or abbreviation function; gratuitous mixed-language wording is not permitted.
8. Do not alter the English lesson payload during a Tamil-only editorial pass.

## Controlled editorial scope
v2.2.2 is a Tamil-language editorial/terminology correction. It does not authorise changes to syllabus coverage, English lessons, lesson order, navigation, Android architecture, permissions, privacy behavior or scientific figure routing.
